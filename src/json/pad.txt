Caravan Cleaning Australia is a family-run Melbourne business that approached me to overhaul their exisiting website. The site had become difficult to maintain and lacked responsiveness across smaller devices, with little consideration for accessibility. 

I began with wireframes in Figma, shaping a more modern design focused on their core services and driving free quote enquiries. The redesign also introduced a before-and-after section to showcase the effectiveness of their cleaning work.

Preserving their existing SEO rankings was a key concern heading into the relaunch, so a mobile-first approach with statically generated pages was a priority from the outset. The final build used AstroJS and React on the front-end, with Sanity.io powering the CMS for easy content management.

Since launch, the business has maintained their #1 Google ranking and seen a strong increase in customer enquiries.
