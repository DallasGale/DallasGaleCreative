Penington Institute is an independent policy research non-profit committed to reducing serious drug harms. As a contracted developer, I built a quiz platform for their Regulate It campaign, an interactive experience designed to show Australians how their views on cannabis regulation compare with the broader population.

The platform was built with AstroJS/React on the front-end, with Google Firebase and Firestore db on the back-end to capture user postcodes on quiz completion and deliver personalised results. Users were encouraged to share their results on social media via a generated share URL, with Cloud Functions handling the dynamic creation of result images.
